# Product Data Columns
* s.no – Serial number of the product entry.
* about_item – Short description of the product.
* asin – Unique Amazon Standard Identification Number.
* availability – Availability status of the product.
* best_sellers_rank – Product ranking in Amazon’s best sellers.
* brand_name – Name of the brand.
* brand_page_url – URL to the brand’s page.
* breadcrumbs – Category navigation trail.
* customer_review_summary – Summary of customer reviews.
* default_variant/0, 1, 2 – Different product variations.
* delivery_date – Expected delivery date.
* fastest_delivery_date – Earliest available delivery date.
* list_price – Original price before discounts.
* manufacturer – Name of the manufacturer.
* model_number – Product model number.
* price_value – Current selling price.
* product_description – Detailed product description.
* product_url – Amazon product page link.
* rating_count – Total number of ratings.
* rating_distribution/1star - 5star – Breakdown of ratings by stars.
* rating_stars – Average product rating.
* recent_purchases – Recent purchase count.
* scrape_time – Timestamp when the data was collected.
* seller_name – Name of the seller.
* seller_page_url – URL to the seller’s page.
* title – Product title.
* all_images – Links to product images.
* rank_1 – Product’s rank in its category.

# Review Data Columns
* s.no – Serial number of the record.
* helpfulVote – Number of users who found the review helpful.
* images/0 … images/7 – Links or placeholders for up to 8 review images.
* productASIN – Amazon Standard Identification Number of the product.
* productVariant – Variant details such as color, size, etc.
* rating – Rating given by the user (1–5 stars).
* reviewID – Unique identifier for the review.
* reviewMetadata – Metadata such as country and date of review.
* reviewPosition – Position/order of the review (if available).
* reviewText – Full textual content of the review.
* reviewTitle – Title/summary of the review.
* reviewURL – Direct URL link to the review on Amazon.
* verifiedPurchase – Whether the purchase was verified (TRUE/FALSE).
* videos/0 – Links or placeholders for video reviews (if any).
* cleaned_review_text – Preprocessed/cleaned version of the review text (lowercased, stopwords removed, etc.).
* sentiment_score – Sentiment score derived from the cleaned review text (range 0–1).
